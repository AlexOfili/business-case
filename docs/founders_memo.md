# Founder's Memo — Is this a business worth building?

**To:** Riverside Malls steering committee
**From:** Pilot product lead
**Re:** Smart trolley pilot — strategic verdict
**Date:** 2026-07-31

---

Is this a business worth building? Yes — but not for the reason most people will tell you.

The unit economics are real. At £200 per trolley per month the pilot clears break-even at 19 trolleys and prints £1,000/month profit on 25. Even 2× support cost holds us at exactly break-even; the defensive case is boring and true. £150 loses money, £250 is upside, £300 is for the second act.

The offensive case is the real prize. A mall that knows how shoppers actually move through it — dwell time, hot zones, basket abandonment moments — owns a signal no e-commerce stack can replicate. That signal becomes the seed of every retailer conversation in the mall for the next decade, and the data compounds faster than the trolleys depreciate.

Build the pilot. Defend the £200 headline. Treat the trolleys as the data-collection layer; sell the data, not the metal.

*(149 words.)*
